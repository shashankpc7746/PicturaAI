      How it works

      1. 1

        Choose a photo

        Upload one of yours or try a sample. JPG, PNG or WEBP up to 25 MB.

      2. 2

        Pick a painting

        Choose a masterpiece from the gallery, upload a painting you own, or describe a style in words.

      3. 3

        Paint it and adjust

        Your picture appears in the frame. Drag the intensity slider until it looks right, then download, share or save it.

      Questions

      Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

      On most computers and recent phones the painting is made in your browser and your photo never leaves your device. On older devices the photo goes to our server, is used for that one picture and is not saved there.

      How big are the downloads?

      You can save the full size, a 1080 pixel wide version for Instagram, or a print size of 1754 pixels on the long side (A4 at 150 dpi). Painting on your device gives the sharpest results.

      Where do the paintings come from?

      Every painting in the gallery is in the public domain. Sources and licences are listed on the credits page. If you upload your own painting, make sure you have the right to use it.

      Does it work offline?

      Not yet. You need a connection to open the studio. After your first painting, the browser keeps the painting engine, so later visits start faster.

      Is it free?

      Yes. There is no sign-up and no watermark.
